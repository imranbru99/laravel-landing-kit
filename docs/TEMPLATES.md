## 2. Using Templates in Filament Admin

### 1-Click Apply to Any Product
1. Open the Admin Panel (`/admin`) and navigate to **Products**.
2. Edit any product and select the **Landing Page** action.
3. Click the **Apply Template** action button in the header.
4. Browse or filter the 107 templates by category or search term.
5. Select a template and click **Apply**.
   - *Note*: An automatic revision of your existing landing page is preserved before the new template sections are imported.

### Exporting & Importing Custom Templates
- **Export**: On any template in the Templates table, click **Export JSON** to download a portable `.json` blueprint.
- **Import**: On the Templates list header, click **Import JSON**, upload your file, and LLK will validate and register your new template into the catalog.
